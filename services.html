Services

Four ways to work together.

A focused offer architecture, not a sprawling menu. Every engagement starts with a conversation about what's actually happening, not a quote in a DM.

01 · DIAGNOSTIC

Diagnostic / working session

A focused session to understand a messy data, reporting, CRM, analytics, automation, or technical problem — and leave with a clearer diagnosis and action plan. Often includes problem framing, a current-state map, priority recommendations, and a short written plan.

02 · DEFINED PROJECT

Defined project

A scoped implementation with a concrete outcome — repairing or redesigning a reporting workflow, building a dashboard, cleaning and reconciling a dataset, implementing digital analytics, automating a manual process, or improving a CRM/data workflow.

03 · TRAINING

Training / workshop

Practical training built around your actual tools and workflows — reporting and dashboard literacy, CRM hygiene, GA4 or digital measurement, practical AI use for staff, SQL fundamentals, or documentation and technical handoff.

04 · FRACTIONAL SUPPORT

Fractional technical/data support

Ongoing help for an organization that needs experienced technical judgment without a full-time hire — monthly office hours, a prioritized technical backlog, troubleshooting and vendor review, reporting oversight, and leadership thought partnership.

On pricing

Scoped to the work, not quoted from a menu.

This is professional consulting, priced against real scope, urgency, and the value at stake — not a discount rate. Every engagement starts with a short conversation about what's happening, what outcome you need, and who's affected, before anything is priced.
